📖 GAME RULES

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Win: Destroy 7 pathogens before they destroy too many organs.

Lose: The Brain is destroyed, OR 5 organs are destroyed.

🔄 TURN ORDER

Each round proceeds:

1. Pathogen Turn — All pathogens move toward organs and attack.

2. Immune Cell 1 Turn — Roll movement, move, attack, play cards.

3. Immune Cell 2 Turn — Same as above.

4. End Round — Effects expire, AI draws a virulence card.

🚶 MOVEMENT

Roll 2d6 + movement modifier to get your tile count.

Click MOVE MODE then click any highlighted (reachable) tile.

Moving to an organ tile ends your movement phase.

New pathogens spawn every 3 rounds on a random spawn point (1–6).

⚔️ COMBAT

To attack a pathogen, be on the same tile and click ATTACK.

Attack roll: d6 + ATK modifier. Pathogens lose HP equal to damage.

Pathogens attack organs automatically when they land on an organ tile.

Pathogens with Bonus Damage roll 2d6 take the higher result vs preferred organ.

🧠 BLOOD-BRAIN BARRIER

Any unit trying to enter the Brain must first land on the BBB Gate and roll d20 ≥ 7.

Failure stops movement at the gate. Some pathogens can bypass the BBB automatically.

🫙 GI TRACT

The pink column is the esophagus / GI Tract. It is one-way downward.

Only pathogens with canUseEsophagus can enter (Giardia, Ascaris, Clonorchis, H. Pylori, etc.).

These pathogens use the GI tract as a shortcut to reach intestines / stomach / pancreas.

☣ SEPTIC SHOCK

When 3 or more organs are destroyed, Septic Shock activates.

All pathogens immediately gain +1 HP, +1 ATK, and +1 MOV permanently.

🃏 PLAYER CARDS

Draw immune support cards and play them on your turn.

Broad Spectrum Antibiotic — Deal 4 damage to any one pathogen.

Diet & Exercise — Heal any organ by 2 HP.

RBC Support — Heal any organ by 3 HP.

Extra Mitochondria — +3 movement this turn.

Membrane Attack Complex — Kill a pathogen with ≤ 3 HP instantly.

Memory B-Cell — Any pathogen of the same type takes +2 damage.

Mitosis — Gain an extra attack phase this turn.

Aortic Highway — Move to any organ tile instantly.

🦠 IMMUNE CELL ABILITIES

B-Cell — After attacking, may place an Antibody marker that halts pathogen movement.

Eosinophil — Deals ×2 damage to parasites.

Helper T-Cell — +1 ATK for each ally on the same tile. Can Mobility Shift for extra turns.

Macrophage — Phagocytosis: destroy a pathogen with ≤ 2 HP without rolling. +1 ATK vs bacteria.

Natural Killer Cell — Rolling a 1 on attack instantly destroys any pathogen (Lethal Precision).

☣ AI VIRULENCE FACTORS

The AI draws and plays virulence cards each round. Played cards appear in Active AI Virulence Factors in the right panel.

Airborne Transmission — All pathogens get +2 MOV this round.

Super Antigen — All pathogens get +2 ATK this round.

Antibiotic Resistance — Chosen pathogen negates the next player attack.

Flagella — Chosen pathogen gets +2 MOV permanently.

Attachment Pili — Chosen pathogen gets +1 ATK permanently.

Cell Wall — Chosen pathogen gets +3 HP permanently.

Anti-Vaxxer — Spawn an additional pathogen immediately.

Latency — Chosen pathogen ignores the next attack against it.

Essential Oils — Deal 2 direct damage to the Brain.
